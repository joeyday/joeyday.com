Established in 2002 by Joey Day, this is an exercise in critical thinking and writing about Christian theology, apologetics, technology, software engineering, web development, video games, ramen noodles, Rubik's cubes, and whatever other topics strike my fancy.

## About the author

[![[2005-03-925250_526362697475828_152184717_n-300x300.jpg|200|right]]](https://instagram.com/p/oq7pHzgFKr/)

Joey has been designing websites since 1998, and has been passionate about [web standards](https://www.w3.org/standards/ "Standards - W3C") since 2003. In 2008 he got a MacBook and an iPhone and hasn’t looked back. He types on the [Dvorak keyboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dvorak_Simplified_Keyboard "Dvorak-Dealey “Simplified” Keyboard") ([but you shouldn’t](https://papers.jday.co/view/dvorak "“The Myth of Dvorak” by Joey Day")) and has been known to dabble in [double-entry bookkeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-entry_bookkeeping_system "Double-entry bookkeeping") (seriously). Since 2012 he has made his living engineering software, variously for a higher-ed non-profit, a mortgage company, and lately for a couple different financial tech companies.[^1]

![[2005-03-umbrella-1024x1024.jpg|200|left]] Though he was raised a sixth-generation member and completed a full-time mission for [The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org), Joey left the LDS faith in 2002 and is now an Evangelical Christian. Sometimes he writes about Mormonism, but he tries his best to treat the subject with candor and fairness.

![[2005-03-Day-2279.png|200|right]] Joey has been married to the charming and beautiful Janene Day since 2005 and together they have two kids, a 16-year-old and a 13-year-old. They worship and fellowship at [Jordan Valley Church](https://jordanvalleychurch.org/ "Jordan Valley Church") in West Jordan, Utah.

If you need to contact Joey for any reason (comments, questions, corrections, concerns, compliments, constructive criticism, scathing insults, etc.), you're welcome to reach out to him on [Mastodon]([https://mastodon.social/@joeyday) or [Facebook](https://facebook.com/joeynday).
