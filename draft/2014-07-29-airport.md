It took several years, but the router apparently absorbed a lot of heat (enough to discolor the plastic on the bottom, as you can see) and finally kicked the bucket this week. The router was a Linksys WRT300N, which served us very well. Cisco/Linksys really is the best when it comes to robust networking hardware. If the rest of this post doesn't convince you to buy Apple networking hardware, you really cannot go wrong buying anything by Linksys.

[caption id="attachment_2385" align="alignright" width="200"]<a href="http://apple.com/airport-extreme"><img src="http://joeyday.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/product_hero_image_2x-640x1024.jpg" alt="Apple AirPort Extreme" width="200" class="size-large wp-image-2385" /></a> Apple AirPort Extreme[/caption] However, after a long history of using Linksys devices, I had been wanting to get an <a href="http://apple.com/airport-extreme" title="Apple AirPort Extreme">Apple AirPort Extreme</a>, and this was just the excuse I needed to finally pull the trigger on that purchase.
