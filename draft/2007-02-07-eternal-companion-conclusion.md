This is the fourth and final post in a series about the Latter-day Saint doctrine of eternal marriage that began on May 27 with “<a href="/2006/05/27/eternal-companion">Eternal Companion: Introduction</a>”.

I promised in a comment on my last installment that the next post in the series would compare marriage with the relationship of Christ and the church.

<h4>Summary</h4>

To recap, let me lay out the three main points I've made in this series:

<ol>
    <li>Marriage ends at death.</li>
    <li>Marriage is not required of all.</li>
    <li>Marriage is a metaphor for our corporate relationship with Christ.</li>
</ol>
