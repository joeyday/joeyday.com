With iOS 8, Apple has done away with Camera Roll. What they had promised to do back at WWDC was replace Camera Roll and Photostream with something new called iCloud Photo Library. If they had actually done this today, I would not be writing this blog post. At some point in the last week or two, though, for reasons not known to the general public, Apple decided they couldn't do it yet. Maybe they were concerned that it wasn't going to scale as well as they had originally hoped? Maybe they decided it would be bad timing to roll out this feature in light of all the controversy surrounding celebritiesâ€™ photos being stolen out of the cloud? Whatever the reason, they have delayed the roll-out of iCloud Photo Library ((They also pulled all the HealthKit apps from the App Store today, citing a critical bug in HealthKit. Iâ€™m guessing some people at Apple have not had a happy day today.)), and what they rolled out instead seems to me entirely half-baked.

What they've done is retained the Photostream, but they went ahead and removed Camera Roll anyway. Yes, there is now a new Recently Added section, but it is very different from Camera Roll. Camera Roll only showed the photos that were actually stored on your device. It never showed photos from the cloud. The Recently Added album shows your most recent photos both from the device and from the cloud. So now there's no easy way to tell which photos are actually stored on your phone and which are stored only in the cloud. That in and of itself isn't a big deal. When you plug your phone into your Mac and launch Image Capture, you still see and have the option to import only the pictures that are stored on your device. However, there's one crucial difference that has me upset.

My process in all previous versions of iOS was to check the box in Image Capture to remove photos after import. After the import, my Camera Roll would be empty, but that was fine since, if I wanted to look at my recent photos, they were always right there in Photostream. Now when I follow the same procedure, the photos disappear from my phone entirely! It seems the photos are still there in Photostream, however, since they still show up on my iPad. Additionally, if I turn Photostream off and back on again on my phone, those photos reappear in my timeline. So, why do they disappear when I tell Image Capture to remove them after import? As long as they're still in the cloud, shouldn't I be able to see them from my phone? It's a mystery.

The way iCloud Photo Library was explained, basically all your photos would be sent to the cloud immediately and retained forever. Whatever photos were actually on your phone were considered to be cached there temporarily, much as music is cached locally when you use iTunes Match. When storage starts to get tight on your phone, the OS is free to delete some of your photos from that temporary cache since it is all stored permanently in the cloud anyway. Those photos might get cached back to the device again if you look at them later, but there's no real reason to keep them on the device, especially if storage is low.

The details of this half-baked solution haven't been explained at all anywhere that I can find (most reviews of iOS 8, even the ones published today, mistakenly report that the iCloud Photo Library feature was released as Apple described it at WWDC and as it existed in the early iOS 8 betas), but I assume the current solution will not do what iCloud Photo Library was advertised to do. I assume, since the same old Photostream is still around, photos are not retained indefinitely in the cloud, but will be deleted after 30 days or when my Photostream hits 1,000 photos, whichever happens first. And I assume the photos that are stored locally are not considered a temporary cache, but will be retained indefinitely. But I don't want them retained indefinitely because they take up space on my device! I want to make sure I have them backed up and then remove them from my phone to free up that space.

So here's my current solution: I've turned off Photostream all together. I already sync my photos to two other cloud services straight from my device: Dropbox (using Carousel) and Picturelife. To backup my photos to my Mac, I use Image Capture as I always have and go ahead and remove the photos from my device during import. I can't browse my older photos in the Photos app, but any attempt to make that work in a semi-normal fashion just feels like a complete hack to me. I'm looking forward to having my photos in iCloud again once iCloud Photo Library finally becomes available. I have no idea how that might force me to rethink my process yet again, but I have every hope it will be better than what I'm doing now.

If you have a better way to do this, please, please, please, leave a comment below and let me know.
