My wife and I have tried a few new drinks lately that I'd like to recommend. The first is <a href="http://www.fresca.com">Fresca Peach Citrus</a>. I've always liked Fresca, but this new flavor is the absolute best soda I've ever tasted. Not that it's the nectar of the gods or anything, but trust me, it's tasty.

The other drink is Welch's White Grape Cherry. I can't really describe it, but my wife and I both really like how well grape and cherry go together.
