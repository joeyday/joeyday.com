As you might have guessed from <a href="http://joeyday.com/2011/09/19/by-the-grace-of-god-they-were-freely-willing/">another recent essay here</a>, I do not believe humans have libertarian free will. I do believe we make choices, but that those choices are always ultimately within the boundaries of God's sovereign control over everything. But I've been thinking about this a lot lately, and trying to understand better why so many Christians believe that libertarian free will is a Biblical concept.

So, if you're a supporter of human free will, I'd love for you to submit scripture verses, passages, chapters, etc., where the Bible seems to directly teach or at least indirectly support the idea. A couple ground rules, though: (1) If you agree with me and you don't believe the Bible teaches free will, then I'd really prefer you not comment. The point here is not to start a Bible bash, I'm just trying to understand the argument from the other side. (2) The point here is to establish support for free will from the Bible. I'm not interested in philosophical arguments or vague inferences. If the Bible really teaches free will, then you should be able to find some pretty explicit references to it right in the text.
