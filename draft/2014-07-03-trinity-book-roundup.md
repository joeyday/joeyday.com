If I had to come up with one criticism of this book it would be that, while it focuses on showing Trinitarianism to be superior to strict monotheism (e.g. Islam, Judaism, Arianism, etc.), it does little if anything to address its relative strengths over against polytheism or pantheism. This is something that's only recently occurred to me as a weakness in many similar books on Trinitarianism.
