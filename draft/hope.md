I’m scared becasue I don’t know what’s true anymore.

I’ve never been really big into studying politics or very passionate about voting for specific candidates. I’m an intellectual person and an armchair academic, I just have other topics and interests I’d rather spend my time and effort studying and understanding. I haven’t voted in many election cycles in recent decades. When people ask me where I am on the political spectrum I say I’m a moderate or independent or centrist. Sometimes I say I’m more conservative than I want to admit and sometimes I say I’m more liberal than I want to admit. When I have voted, I’ve voted for both republican and democratic candidates in about equal proportion.

I would say rather than being a party voter I’m an issues voter. I used to think I understood many political issues and had some strongly-held opinions alongside more weakly-held ones.

But I don’t know what I believe anymore. I’m terrified by the current political climate in the United States and I think both sides in our two-party system are equally to blame.

## Context

For context if you’re reading this years from now, we’re one year into Donald Trump’s second term of office as President of the United States. He was legally and legitimately elected to this position and he campaigned on the promise, among many other things, of a mass deportation of unauthorized migrants.

Now he’s doing exactly what he promised and what the majority of Americans indicated through their voting rights that they wanted. He’s roughly doubled the size of the U.S. Immigrations and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agency by recruiting and training new officers, most of whom are local residents of the cities and states they’re operating in, but some of whom are also transferred into cities from outside.

There’s a pretty big campaign going on in Minneapolis right now where ICE is pretty active detaining and deporting migrants. The administration says they’re focusing on the most violent criminals, and for my part I believe them. I believe they believe (from the administration level all the way down to individual ICE officers) that what they’re doing for our country is right and just and is the will of the majority of the citizenry.

I read from an independent news source that the Biden administration carried about more deportations in its last year than the second Trump administration carried out in its first year.


I’m scared.

I don’t know if what’s happening right now is being orchestrated by some shadowy conspiracy on the right or some shadowy conspiracy on the left, some combination of both working separately or both working together, or if it’s just an emergent property of the really complex system we’ve made for ourselves. I don’t know which of these scenarios seems most plausible and, frankly, I don’t know which of them scares me more. I just know I’m scared and I don’t trust anything I’m seeing, hearing, or reading anywhere, full stop.

Certainly there are lies, propaganda, and brainwashing coming from both the left and the right. Certainly our algorithmic timelines are insane feedback loops that, based on our subtle behaviors (liking or subscribing to people and content, clicking or not clicking on links, dwelling a few seconds longer on a video or quickly dismissing it), either amplify and echo back to us what they think we prefer or de-emphasize and hide what they think we don’t prefer. The internet had so much promise to tear down inherent bias in the media by democratizing information and freeing it from institutions, putting it in the hands of the people, but it seems like it’s just made everything so much worse.

I would say to be responsible citizens we should seek news and opinions from outside our echo chamber, but wherever we go we’ll just see the way the truth (if we can even call it truth anymore) has been warped and twisted by a different feedback loop. This is why I don’t know what to believe anymore and why I’m so terrified.

I remain confident in only one thing and this is the bedrock I’m falling on now: There is a loving God who has a benevolent plan for the world. Good will prevail in the end. This is the only place hope can come from in such a hopeless world. I’m praying for my country and all of you my neighbors, friends, and family. I love you.





To be responsible adults living today we need to actively seek out news and opinions outside our echo chambers (the problem here is the sources outside our echo chambers are just telling the other side’s lies; who can we trust??), challenge our own understanding of the facts and revise our assumptions and conclusions constantly. And if/when we do form responsible strongly-held opinions, we need to restrain ourselves from vilifying those who hold  different opinions. So many Americans hate each other right now and we need to de-escalate this conflict before it becomes a civil war.
