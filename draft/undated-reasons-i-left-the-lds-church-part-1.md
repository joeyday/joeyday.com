I recently had a conversation with a good friend who is LDS. We were talking about what it means to be a Christian and I explained that I believe there are Mormons who are my brothers and sisters in Christ. They aren't Christians because they belong to any particular church or religious system, but simply because they know Christ.

He responded by asking me this question: "If Mormons can have a relationship with Christ, why did you feel you had to leave the church? Why couldn't you continue to develop your relationship with Christ and remain Mormon?"

I thought that was an interesting question that deserved a straightforward answer.
