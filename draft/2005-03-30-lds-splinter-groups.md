Steve, I think Terry was merely pointing out what he perceives to be a double standard (though I suppose I shouldn't put words in his mouth like this).

I think for many Mormons, denominationalism is a sticking point. Mormons will happily point out that there are thousands of different Christian denominations, claiming that Christians are lost in every wind of doctrine. They are less apt to admit that Mormons are just as widely differentiated in their beliefs.

I heard an interesting statistic the other day. I have no idea how much truth there is to it, so I hesitate to even post it. There are something close to a hundred different Mormon splinter groups claiming direct descent from the church Smith founded in 1830. Since there have been less than 200 years since Mormonism's inception, the existence of so many splinters indicates that Mormonism is splintering faster than Christianity has over the past 2,000 years.

There are so many other factors concerning this "statistic" that haven't been taken into consideration, but I think it at least illustrates one valid point. If Mormons are going to claim they are the true church because they are unified on doctrine, they need to take a cold hard look at how unified they really are.
