Dan Benjamin of [Hivelogic](http://www.hivelogic.com) recently wrote [a post](http://hivelogic.com/articles/2006/04/17/setup_part_ii) about his own software setup. Even though he uses a Mac and none of the software he mentioned would be of any use to me, I was intrigued by the idea and inspired to write a similar post of my own.

<!--more-->

Dan actually wrote two posts, starting with one about his hardware setup. I'm not sure I could fill up a whole post talking about my hardware, so I think a paragraph will do. For the past couple of months I've been using a company-issued [HP Compaq tc4200 Tablet PC](http://h10010.www1.hp.com/wwpc/us/en/sm/WF05a/321957-304452-306995-304455-306995-457948.html). I was a bit skeptical at first about the whole tablet thing, but I must say it adds another layer of freedom to personal computing. I always have the option to flip the lid up and use it like a normal laptop, but it's quite useful being able to take notes and refer to my documents or the web while I'm up walking around.

<h4>Applications</h4>

- Firefox — extensions, developer stuff
- Thunderbird — feedreader
- EditPad Pro — regular expressions
- SmartFTP
- iTunes

- OneNote
- GIMP 2, but lately Macromedia Fireworks 8
