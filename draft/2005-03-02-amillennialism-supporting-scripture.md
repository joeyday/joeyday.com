I started a <a href="/2004/09/24/a-case-for-amillennialism-part-1">series</a> about <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eschatology">eschatology</a> (specifically about one view of eschatology called <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amillennialism">Amillennialism</a>) over a year ago and never continued it. The subject came up again recently with a co-worker and I've consequently revisited many of the scriptures I had studied back then. My original post was prompted by a book I read called <cite><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?tag=joeyday-20%26link_code=xm2%26camp=2025%26creative=165953%26path=http://www.amazon.com/gp/redirect.html%253fASIN=080106435X%2526tag=joeyday-20%2526lcode=xm2%2526cID=2025%2526ccmID=165953%2526location=/o/ASIN/080106435X%25253FSubscriptionId=09XQMBPM9EDAPGEVZ3R2" title="View product details at Amazon">A Case for Amillennialism</a></cite>. I've referred back to that book in the present study (which still has my little post-it flags for sections I had intended to blog about), but have tried not to rely on it, preferring to keep to the scriptures themselves as much as possible.

I think one reason I never finished the series was I got too ambitious about what I wanted to say. So, I'd like to try and finish out the series with a few brief posts that touch on distinct aspects of the Amillennial position.

<!--more-->

In a nutshell (and quoting from the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Amillennialism&oldid=34601844">Wikipedia article</a> here), Amillennialism is "an interpretation of chapter 20 of the Book of Revelation which sees the '1000-year reign' of Christ as non-literal, figurative or symbolic." I'd like to start -- or rather, continue -- this series by examining a few passage of scripture outside of Revelation first. If there really is a literal millennium between Christ's second coming and the end of all things, we would expect to see it mentioned or at least alluded to elsewhere in scripture besides the book of Revelation.

- Matt. 24
- 1 Cor. 15
- 1 Thes. 4:13-18, 5:1-11
- 2 Pet. 3
