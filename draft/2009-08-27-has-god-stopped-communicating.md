A few months ago I bookmarked a post on a blog called Mormanity by Jeff Lindsay. The post, titled "<a href="http://mormanity.blogspot.com/2005/11/questions-for-fellow-christians-to.html">Questions for Fellow Christians to Consider: Why Would God Stop Communicating with Man?</a>", attempts to show the absurdity of the Evangelical notion that God has ceased to communicate with man. I had meant to blog about Lindsay's post, but never got around to it. I was about to delete the bookmark feeling that the time had passed for me to really interact with it, but, after surveying the comments and realizing that no one from the evangelical community had really weighed in, I felt I should still offer my thoughts to the discussion.

I believe Lindsay has erected something of a straw man in his arguments. I honestly don't think you can find an Evangelical who believes God doesn't communicate with man in some way or another even today. Now, there are some evangelicals who call themselves <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessationism">Cessationists</a> (I am not one of them, though I wouldn't call myself a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charismatic_Movement">Charismatic</a> either), meaning they believe the spiritual gifts of prophecy, tongues, healing, etc. have been removed from the earth. However, even Cessationists must believe that the Holy Spirit dwells in them, teaching them and leading them into all truth, and thus that God still communicates with man.

I would go so far as to say God is communicating more with man in this age (which had its beginning at Pentecost) than in any other age of the world.

- 1 Pet. 1:11; 2 Pet. 1:21; 2 Sam. 23:2; Acts 1:16 -- Prophets spoke by the spirit
- John 7:39 -- Spirit not given yet
- John 14:26 -- Spirit will teach us all things
- Joel 2:28-32; Acts 2 -- Spirit poured out at Pentecost
- Dan. 9:24 -- Seal vision and prophecy
- Heb. 1:1-2 -- In last days God has spoken to us through Christ
- Jer. 31:31-34; 1 John 2:27 -- No need of a teacher
- 1 Cor. 12 -- Spiritual gift of prophecy
