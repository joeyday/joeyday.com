If you had asked me a few days ago if I was a <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessationism">cessationist</a>, I probably would've answered in the negative. I would've told you I'm an anti-cessationist, or the positive equivalent, a continuationist. I had a good conversation with a couple LDS friends the other day about it, which led to the reading of the afore-linked Wikipedia article where I was forced to concede that my belief lies somewhere on the spectrum between cautious continuationist and concentric cessationist.

If you've never heard of cessationism, you're probably wondering what I'm talking about. As the Wikipedia article states: "cessationism is the view that the charismatic gifts of the Holy Spirit, such as tongues, prophecy and healing, ceased being practiced early on in Church history."
