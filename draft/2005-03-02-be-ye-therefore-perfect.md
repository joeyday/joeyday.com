I watched a good video last week called <em>Speaking the Truth in Love to Mormons</em>. It features Pastor Mark Cares, and is distributed by his ministry, appropriately called <a href="http://www.truthinlovetomormons.com">Speaking the Truth in Love to Mormons</a>. The video was fairly simplistic, and gave only a brief introduction to Mormonism (a subject that could fill volumes).

In 40 minutes, the video shows four or five role-playing situations involving an Evangelical Christian family and two missionaries from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

The simple premise of the video is Mormonism's teaching that men must perfect themselves before they can qualify for eternal life. LDS missionaries frequently cite Matthew 5:48 to defend their position that men are commanded to make themselves perfect. All men, according to LDS doctrine, must work to make (or to keep) themselves worthy of exaltation ((It should be noted that in Mormonism, exaltation and salvation are differentiated. (Continue explaining concept later))).

<blockquote>"Be ye therefore perfect, even as your father in heaven is perfect."</blockquote>

Pastor Cares shows a simple way to show Mormons that their doctrine is not in line with Biblical teaching. Hebrews 10:14
