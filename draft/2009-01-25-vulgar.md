There's a controversy between fundamentalist and non-fundamentalist conservative Christians about which translations of the Bible are trustworthy and authoritative. Many fundamentalists trust the King James Version and only the King James Version ((It should be noted that, although some of what I'm saying here might be accurately pointed at Latter-day Saints, I'm not necessarily targeting them here. The LDS don't use the KJV because they believe it is the most correct, trustworthy, or authoritative translation. Quite to the contrary, they hold that it's full of transmission errors---they erroneously label them "translation errors"---and is therefore only marginally accurate. As I understand it, their KJV-only stance is held more for uniformity, convenience, and tradition than for any other reason. Additionally, most modern translations are chartered by evangelical or other groups Latter-day Saints understandably distrust. If the LDS Church ever accepts a modern translation of the Bible, I imagine it would be one commissioned and translated by and for their own people, much as Jehovah's Witnesses use and rely only upon the New World Translation. Of course, the NWT is generally considered inferior by mainstream Christianity and any translation created by the LDS Church would likely get the same treatment, which is probably why they haven't gone down that road. But I digress.)), while non-fundamentalists prefer modern translations such as the New American Standard Bible, Revised Standard Version, or English Standard Version. These new translations bring the text of Bible into more common speech, eschewing the "thees" and "thous" of the Jacobian English employed by the King James. Those groups who hold to the King James claim that the Jacobian English is more formal and gives more reverence to God and the subjects discussed in the Bible. Many of these groups pray in a Jacobian style and consider it more reverent and respectful to God than casually spoken personal prayers.

<a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koine_Greek">Koine Greek</a> is the language the New Testament was originally written in.
