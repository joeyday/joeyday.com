<em><a href="http://www.joeyday.org/2005/03/02/the-symbol-of-the-cross#comment-55">Terry</a> asked:</em>

<blockquote>What is the definition of an evangelical, as it seems to be used often by you and those who comment? What is a true Christian, as many in America claim to be Christian?</blockquote>

I'm going to get raked over the coals for answering this question, but I think it deserves a response.

<!--more-->

To answer your first question, "Evangelical" is a word I use to qualify what kind of Christian I am. "Protestant" is another popular word used for the same purpose.

Having come from Mormonism, I am probably a lot more liberal with my terms than many Christians might be. I don't like to make contrasts between "Mormons" and "Christians", for the simple reason that I don't wish to alienate or offend my Mormon friends. Perhaps there is no way to preach the gospel without offending some, but I take seriously the charges given in scripture to speak the truth with meekness, respect and love ((Galatians 4:5-6; 2 Timothy 2:23-26; Titus 3:1-11; 1 Peter 3:15-16)).

In terms of classifying their religious tradition, I try to give Mormons the benefit of the doubt and consider Mormonism a branch of Christianity. In this sense, I might be inclined to call a Mormon friend an LDS Christian or a Mormon Christian, though you will probably never hear me do that. In the same way, I am an Evangelical Christian or a Protestant Christian. In this particular sense of the word "Christian," I do not believe all Christians (whether Protestant, Evangelical, or Mormon) belong to Christ or will have a citizenship in heaven. Many of those who belong to a Christian tradition or are members of a Christian church are not true Christians.

What do I mean by "true" Christian? I believe a "true" Christian (I would also call this person a "born-again" Christian) is someone who has fulfilled the commandment given in Matthew 5:48: "Be perfect, therefore, as your heavenly Father is perfect." In this sense, as much as I would like to, I cannot call Mormons Christians.

You'll most likely ask me how anyone could possibly attain to perfection.
