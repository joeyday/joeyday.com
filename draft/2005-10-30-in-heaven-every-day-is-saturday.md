<blockquote>There remains, then, a Sabbath-rest for the people of God; for anyone who enters God's rest also rests from his own work, just as God did from his. (Hebrews 4:9-10)</blockquote>

In the garden, Adam and Eve weren't required to do any work for their living. First of all, there were no weeds. The trees and bushes produced fruit of their own volition. They didn't have to be planted a certain way or watered. Everything took care of itself. Only after the fall was Adam told he would eat of the ground "by the sweat of his brow."

There is so much made today of the journey I fear people are largely unaware of the destination. God promises there will be rest from labor in heaven.
