<h4>to the producers and distributors of the <cite>Search for the Truth</cite> video</h4>

I'm apprehensive as I begin this letter. There are more than a few of those who have helped distribute this video who I consider to be close friends. What I am about to say is in no way meant to hurt or offend you. I am merely doing what I believe the Holy Spirit demands that I do in Christian love.

I think you've made a huge mistake. You don't understand members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints like I do, and I believe that by making and distributing this video you've done more harm than good. I cannot sit idly by while you make the good news of Jesus Christ even uglier than it already is to Latter-day Saints. You are not speaking the truth in love. You are spreading lies and deception out of fear of something you don't understand.

Don't get me wrong. I am not defending Mormonism. I agree that Mormonism is itself a great deception. However, I further believe there's a way to say that to Latter-day Saints while at the same time giving them the respect and the love that Christ commands we show them.



Sure, you may pick off a few of the weak ones, but many of those people will be just as weak in your churches as they were in the LDS church. These are not God's elect, but the ones who are "tossed to and fro by the waves and carried about by every wind of doctrine" (Ephesians 4:14). Soon enough they'll leave your church when they get tired of it and see a flashy promo video for Christian Scientology or some other such nonsense.



The worst thing about your video is I honestly can't find the gospel in it. Surely I don't need to remind you that the gospel is "the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes" (Romans 1:16).

> For the word of the cross is folly to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God. For it is written,

Where is the one who is wise? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of the world? For since, in the wisdom of God, the world did not know God through wisdom, it pleased God through the folly of what we preach to save those who believe. For Jews demand signs and Greeks seek wisdom, but we preach Christ crucified, a stumbling block to Jews and folly to Gentiles, but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God and the wisdom of God. For the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and the weakness of God is stronger than men.

By focusing on the lesser things like Joseph Smith's comments about Quakers on the moon, you're shooting yourselves in the foot. It's not the wisdom of this world that convinces anybody, but the Holy Spirit. By failing to preach the gospel undiluted, you have failed the Latter-day Saint people.

> How then will they call on him in whom they have not believed? And how are they to believe in him of whom they have never heard? And how are they to hear without someone preaching?



Latter-day Saints have a deeply embedded defense mechanism Pastor Mark Cares likes to call a "persecution complex". They see themselves in a very real way as a persecuted people (cf. Matthew 10:16-42), and when something like your DVD distribution happens, they see it, paradoxically, as one more sure indication that Mormonism is true. No matter how convincing your arguments are, if they aren't delivered in a spirit of meekness and of total love and concern, they will be written off as the work of Satan that cannot and will not prevail against Christ's church. I don't think you can get meekness, love, and concern out of a mass-produced DVD, no matter how attractively you package it.

Jesus never said lie or spread deception about your enemies. He said to love them.
