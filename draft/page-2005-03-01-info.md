<h2>About Joey Day : Foundation</h2>

Hello! My name is Joey. Welcome to my <em>Foundation</em> weblog. Here you'll find a collection of ramblings about Christian theology, faith, and current events.

Though I was raised a sixth-generation member and completed a full-time mission for <a href="http://www.lds.org">The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints</a>, I left the LDS faith around May of 2002 and now consider myself an Evangelical Protestant Christian. I don't claim to be an expert in theology, but it is a subject of great interest to me. I pray that you'll find the content here informative and faith-promoting.

In seeking to clarify and refine my own understanding of God's truth, I often start from the basis of what I used to believe, namely <a href="http://www.mormon.org">Mormonism</a>. Naturally, this tendency will express itself in my writing. To believing Latter-day Saints it should be clearly stated that I mean no offense by any of the thoughts I post here. I'm not deliberately targeting you or the Mormon faith; I'm simply telling my story from the framework that makes the most sense to me. If something I've said is incorrect or offensive in any way, please contact me or leave a comment and I'll do my best to make things right.

Perhaps my one claim to fame is that I'm the creator and proprietor of the <a href="http://www.hrwiki.org">Homestar Runner Wiki</a>, a collaborative online knowledge-base dedicated to the <a href="http://www.homestarrunner.com">Homestar Runner</a> Flash cartoons created by Mike and Matt Chapman.
