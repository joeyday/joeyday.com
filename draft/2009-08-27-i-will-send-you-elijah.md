I read more of the Christmas story than usual this year, paying closer attention than I think I ever have to the story of Zachariah and the events surrounding John the Baptist's birth as recorded in Luke 1:5-25, 57-80. I also heard a wonderful sermon on the prophecy of Simeon when he beheld the Christ-child as recorded in Luke 2:25-35, <cite><a href="http://www.sermonaudio.com/sermoninfo.asp?sermonID=12200611713">My Eyes Have Seen Your Salvation</a></cite> by Jonathan McGreevy of <a href="http://www.tenth.org">Tenth Presbyterian Church</a> in Philadelphia.

I found a line in the angel's prophecy to Zechariah that brought back a subject I studied at some length a while ago but never blogged about, the nature and identity of the Elijah who was to come as prophesied in Malachi 4:5-6.

- Isa. 40:3
- Mal. 3:1
- Mal. 4:5-6

- Luke 1:16-17
- Matt. 11:7-15
- Matt. 17:10-13 (cf. Mark 9:11-13)

- 2 Kings 1:8
- Matt 3:4
- Mark 1:6

- But John himself denied his connection to Elijah: John 1:19-21
