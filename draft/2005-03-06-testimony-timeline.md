9/4/1987 : I am baptized and confirmed a member of the LDS church
4/26/1998 : I receive my patriarchal blessing
10/?/1998 : I experience the temple ordinances for the first time
11/?/1998 : I deliver my missionary farewell talk on the subject of Matthew 10
11/?/1998 : I enter the MTC to train for service in the Kentucky Louisville Mission
6/?/1999 : A Christian missionary in Nicholasville gives me a Strong's Concordance
6/?/1999 : Elder Baker almost leaves the church
? : Elder MacKinnon attempts to go home because of discouragement
? : I cast lots with a stick one Saturday night, ending up in the home of a Pastor who is making last minute preparations for his sermon the next morning
? : I am priviledged to work in the Louisville temple hanging paintings and wall placards
? : The temple is dedicated; all the missionaries attend an endowment ceremony
? : I encounter a girl who gives me a Revised English Bible
? : My faith in God wavers, but Elder Wyatt helps me solidify it
? : My companion and I teach a couple who is simultaneously meeting with some of Jehovah's Witnesses; the couple gives me a copy of the New World Translation which my district leader discards a few weeks later because "missionaries shouldn't keep those kinds of things"
? : My companion and I encounter a girl in Corydon, Indiana who is one of Jehovah's Witnesses; she tells us that persecution is one reason she feels it is true; I realize for the first time that religious persecution is not necessarily a sign that you are walking in the truth
? : President Hatton (branch president) tells of two families who moved into the branch within a few months of each other; one family said they knew they were meant to be there because the moment they moved in horrible things started to happen (Satan must have been opposing them); the other family knew they were meant to be there because the moment they moved in good things began to happen (God began to bless them); I realize for the first time that circumstance is not necessarily a sign that you are walking in the truth
? : Through several encounters with Christians over the course of my mission, I learn and come to appreciate the Christian interpretation of several scriptures
12/8/2000 : I return home honorably from my mission
? : I am called to serve as a Primary teacher
6/7/2001 : Janene and I have our first date
9/5/2001 : I break up with Janene over religious differences
9/?/2001 : I attend an evening service (part of a series on Mormonism) at Southeast; I become acquainted with Tim Clark
12/?/2001 : Janene and I start reading Romans together
1/1/2002 : My ward rotates to a 1pm meeting schedule, allowing me to attend Southeast every Sunday
1/?/2002 : I am released from service as a Primary teacher
? : I attend my last endowment session with Jeremy Howlett
1/28/2002 : I attend a Newcomers' Fellowship at Southeast by myself; I encounter Tim Clark there for the second time
4/?/2002 : Without Janene's knowledge, I write a letter to my parents explaining my intent to leave the church; I pray fervently that God will comfort me during this difficult situation; simultaneously, Janene attends a Sunday School class fellowship, where she (thinking she's betraying my confidence) explains to the entire class that I am LDS; the class agrees to pray for me; when I hear this story later (and when Janene hears of the letter to my parents), we both agree that this was not coincidental
4/30/2002 : The Bishop learns I'm having serious doubts about the church, but (assuming I haven't committed any great sin) feels it's important for me to attend my sister's wedding; I enter the temple one last time
5/?/2002 : I've been thinking about it, mulling it over, still holding back, but on this day I trust Jesus as my Savior for the first time
2003 : I meet with Pastor Mike to fill out a membership record and become a member of Southeast Baptist Church
7-8?/2003 : I tell my parents that I'm thinking of getting baptized at Southeast; we have an hour-long discussion in which they say, understandably, that they cannot possibly support me in my decision; that night I am overcome by the situation and go to my scriptures for support; the Lord directs me back to Matthew 10
8/24/2003 : I am baptized by Pastor Tim Clark
