<h4>What do you mean, controversy?</h4>

Before I get into the meat of this review, let me explain briefly what it means to say there is a "King James Only" controversy. Let me explain up front that we're not talking about Mormonism here. Yes, Mormons use only the King James version of the Bible, but they do not exclude the possibility of better translations being made, and they certainly do not believe the King James is infallible or directly God-breathed.

King James Onlyism is a movement that centers around the notion that the King James translators were inspired by God in such a way as to make the King James version the only true Bible version. As White puts it, "A person who would dare to defend, or even use, another translation of the Bible is, for these people, rejecting the 'true' and 'real' Bible, and by doing so is rejecting the 'true Word of God.'"

King James Only people love to produce literature showing all the "errors" and "omissions" in the NIV, NASB, and other modern translations. In my opinion, this is a great example of "zeal without knowledge" ((Rom. 10:2)).
