Perhaps the most important book I've ever read is <cite><a href="http://amzn.to/hMOXsI?tag=joeyday-20">Desiring God: Meditations of a Christian Hedonist</a></cite> by Pastor John Piper of Bethlehem Baptist Church in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Multnomah Books has just published a revised edition of the book for its 25th anniversary, and along with this publication they have finally made the title available for Amazon's Kindle and other eBook readers. I've already snatched up my copy and plan to read it again sometime this year.
